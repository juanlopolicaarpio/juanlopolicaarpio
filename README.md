## Confidentiality

These are public releases of systems informed by real products I have built and shipped. Company names, customer records, credentials, internal prompts, proprietary rules, and business data are not published. Interactive demos use fictional organizations and synthetic data; selected systems are represented as architecture case studies where publishing source code would reveal sensitive implementation details.

That boundary preserves the organizations that trusted me with their systems while still making the engineering decisions and product capabilities inspectable.
